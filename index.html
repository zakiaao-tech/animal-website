The little lamb

My website will refer to about lambs.

Lambs are universally adored for their oversized, expressive eyes and soft, woolly coats that make them look like living stuffed animals. Their playful nature adds to their charm, as they frequently bounce, skip, and wag their tails when excited.

About lambs

  • They live on Farmlands and pastures alongside their mothers and the rest of the flock.
  • Their diet depend entirely on milk from ewes for vital nutrients.
  • Upon birth they create strong bonds, they recognize their mother instantly by sight, sound, and smell
